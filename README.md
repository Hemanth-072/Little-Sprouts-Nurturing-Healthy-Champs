Simulated Agile Project Management | Jan 2024 – Apr 2024

 

 Project Overview

A hands-on simulation of Agile project management to design a pediatric obesity prevention mobile application. Managed a $27,500 budget across initiation, planning, execution, monitoring, and closure phases—applying iterative sprints, risk analysis, stakeholder engagement, and cross-functional collaboration.

 Objectives & Scope

Aim: Simulate an end-to-end Agile project to combat childhood obesity through a mobile app concept.

Goals:

Educate caregivers on healthy habits via app features.

Incorporate AI-driven personalized recommendations.

Produce comprehensive project management artifacts.

Scope Includes: Project charter, stakeholder analysis, WBS, budget, communication plan, work plan, risk register, monitoring & evaluation, and project closure report.

Out of Scope: Actual app development, code implementation, and live deployment.

 Agile methodologies  Framework

Sprint Duration: 2 weeks each (6 sprints total)

Ceremonies:

Sprint Planning & Backlog Refinement

Daily Stand‑ups (simulated)

Sprint Reviews & Retrospectives

Artifacts: Product backlog, sprint backlog, burn‑down chart, risk register.
